MossGate Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

MossGate is a pixel-art role-playing game you can play alone or alongside other players online. This policy explains what the game does with information.

What we collect

MossGate has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We never sell or share information for advertising.

Your save data

Your character, progress and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us. If you use the in-game “Export save” feature, the save code or file you create stays with you, and you choose where to put it.

Online play

When you are connected to the internet, the game signs in to Google Firebase with an anonymous ID so you can share zones with other players. No name, email address, phone number or contact list is used for this. While you play, the following is shared with other players in the same place and stored temporarily on Firebase servers:

  • the name you gave your character, its level, appearance and equipment;
  • where your character is in the zone and what it is doing (moving, attacking, downed);
  • combat events in that zone, such as damage to creatures, and the preset phrases you choose to say;
  • whether your in-game house is open to visitors, and how it is furnished.

This live information is deleted when you leave the zone or close the game. If you reach a new record in the Veinway Deep, your character name, deepest floor and level are kept on a public leaderboard linked to your anonymous ID.

Firebase is operated by Google. Google may process technical information such as your IP address to provide the connection; see Firebase privacy and security. All data is sent encrypted.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes your save and your anonymous ID. To have a leaderboard entry removed, contact us at the address below.

Internet use

The app also downloads game updates and the pixel font when a connection is available. The game works offline after its first launch; online play simply turns off.

Children

MossGate does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Players communicate only through preset phrases, never free text.

Changes

If online features change what is shared, this policy will be updated before those features launch.
