MossGate Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

MossGate is a single-player pixel-art role-playing game. This policy explains what the game does with information.

What we collect

Nothing. MossGate does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Your save data

Your character, progress and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us. If you use the in-game “Export save” feature, the save code or file you create stays with you, and you choose where to put it.

Internet use

The app downloads game updates and the pixel font from the internet when a connection is available. These requests contain no personal information. The game also works offline after its first launch.

Children

MossGate does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Future online features

If online features such as co-op play or leaderboards are added, this policy will be updated before they launch to describe exactly what is shared.
