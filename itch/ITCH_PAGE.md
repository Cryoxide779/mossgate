## Description

The fortress-city of MossGate fell in a single night. Its seals opened from the inside, its roads broke, and whatever was held beneath it got out. You arrive on the Ash Road with the survivors, bound for CrestHollow and whatever is left to save.

MossGate is a hand-crafted pixel-art side-scrolling RPG with no classes. You become what you practice.

**Seven regions, nearly a hundred places.** Walk from the river crossings of CrestHollow through the spirit-haunted Whisper Woods, the forges of Stonebridge, the islands of Misty Fen Lake, the drowning towers of Marrowbog and the frontier forts of the Old Watchtower, to the crystal wound of the Sundering Scar.

**No class lock-in.** Choose one of ten starting kits, then train any of more than twenty disciplines. Blades, spears, shields, bows, nature magic, necromancy, armour, gathering and crafting all grow with use.

**Real combat.** Roll through attacks, guard with stamina, and time a parry to stagger enemies. Break an enemy's poise to stun it. Every region ends in legendary fights: an Elder Treant, a Bog Leviathan, a Shard Titan.

**Not everything has to die.** Befriend almost any creature with the food it likes and it becomes a companion that levels up and learns commands. Shear wool from mountain goats. Watch the Wavecrest Stag without disturbing it. Mend a dying spirit stag with a crystal instead of a blade.

**Choices that stick.** Decide the fate of a drowned laboratory's formula, a tear in the Veil, and the wound at the heart of the Scar.

**Gather, craft and build.** Every region has its own materials (ashwood, elderwood, copper, driftwood, bogstone, slate, hollowglass and more) and its own crafted gear with its own character. Furnish a home in Hearthrow. Take contracts and freight runs for the towns.

**The Veinway Deep.** A hundred-floor dungeon beneath CrestHollow with a guardian every ten floors.

Plays in your browser on desktop or phone. No ads, no accounts, no data collection. Your progress is saved in your browser; use Menu → Export save to back it up.

Free updates add new regions, story and features over time.
