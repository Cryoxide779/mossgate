THE FALL OF MOSSGATE

STUDIO EDITION

PROPOSED TERRITORY BIBLE

Zone Creatures and Materials

Seven Proposed Regions • Recruitable Species • Resource Ecosystems

Document Control

This bible extends the seven proposed map packages with zone-specific
creature rosters, recruitable companion paths, renewable and recovery
materials, processing chains, and resource-map requirements. All names
and rules remain Proposed until approved through Studio Governance.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  CREATURE INVARIANT
  Every creature in this document has at least one species-appropriate
  recruitment route. Recruitment, rescue, trust, sanctuary, repair,
  cleansing, contract, migration support, and ritual partnership are
  valid paths. Creature materials do not require killing by default.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Shared Creature Tiers

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Tier                                Production Meaning
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Common                              Frequently observed; supports
                                      ecology, learning, professions, and
                                      early recruitment.

  Uncommon                            Localized or behaviorally
                                      specialized; requires knowledge,
                                      equipment, or relationship work.

  Rare                                Limited habitat, unusual
                                      capability, or complex recruitment
                                      chain.

  Legendary                           Region-defining individual or
                                      species; recruitment is a major
                                      quest, accord, restoration, or
                                      long-term bond rather than a loot
                                      reward.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Shared Material Rules

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Rule                                Requirement
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Source                              Every material has a physical,
                                      biological, magical, historical, or
                                      recovered source.

  Method                              Harvesting states whether gathering
                                      is renewable, nonlethal,
                                      recovery-based, agricultural,
                                      salvaged, mined, or restricted.

  Processing                          Raw resources require named
                                      facilities, skills, tools, and
                                      risks.

  Use                                 Materials support specific
                                      components, structures, medicine,
                                      trade, rituals, or creature
                                      equipment.

  Lifecycle                           Condition, repair, contamination,
                                      recycling, reuse, and end-of-life
                                      are defined.

  Provenance                          Rare, historical, creature-derived,
                                      Sacred, corrupted, and Shard
                                      materials retain source records.

  Geography                           Extraction, habitats, processing,
                                      storage, trade, smuggling,
                                      restriction, depletion, and
                                      restoration appear on zone maps.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

GLD-CR-01 • Sunmane Courser (Common)

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Field                               Definition
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Body Plan and Signature             Tall equine body, broad lungs,
                                      golden mane, dark lower legs, and
                                      heat-shedding coat.

  Habitat                             Open prairie, ranch margins, and
                                      protected herd corridors.

  Behavior                            Social, alert, migratory, and
                                      reluctant to leave familiar herd
                                      structures.

  Gameplay Functions                  Mounted travel, caravan scouting,
                                      racing, herding, and weather
                                      sensing.

  Recruitment Path                    Trust through herd assistance,
                                      rescue from wire or fire, ranch
                                      apprenticeship, or adoption from a
                                      sanctuary.

  Bond and Companion Roles            Mount, scout, courier, herd guide,
                                      and agricultural partner.

  Equipment Compatibility             Saddle, light barding, cargo
                                      panniers, signal tack, and
                                      heat-safe harness.

  Renewable Materials                 Brushed mane fiber, shed coat,
                                      manure, and voluntary blood samples
                                      for veterinary research.

  Recovery Materials                  Lawfully recovered hide, hoof, and
                                      bone only after natural death or
                                      authorized ecological recovery.

  Risks and Care                      Requires social contact, large
                                      grazing space, hoof care, and
                                      gradual load training.

  World-State Response                Moves with drought and fire;
                                      restored migration routes improve
                                      health and recruitment.

D. Material Geography

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Map Layer                           Required Placement
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Production Belt                     Map farms, ranches, groves, mills,
                                      tanneries, kilns, ropeworks,
                                      storage, and markets.

  Migration Layer                     Wildherd, crane, roc, and Colossus
                                      corridors with seasonal dates.

  Fire Layer                          Fuel density, firebreaks, wells,
                                      lookout points, evacuation routes,
                                      and burn history.

  Water Layer                         Irrigation, floodplain, ponds,
                                      drinking access, drought priority,
                                      and contamination.

  Trade Layer                         Grain, wool, leather, wagon, salt,
                                      and tool flows toward neighboring
                                      regions.

E. Regional Production Chains

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Chain                               Flow and Regional Function
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Grain and Straw                     Farm → mill → food market; straw →
                                      panel yard, bedding, fuel, and
                                      compost.

  Wool                                Ranch → wash house → spinning hall
                                      → cloth, felt, armor padding, and
                                      blankets.

  Sunwood                             Managed grove → saw yard →
                                      seasoning → wagon, bow, structure,
                                      and repair shops.

  Stormglass                          Storm field → protected collection
                                      → glassworks → lens and
                                      weather-instrument production.

  Creature Partnership                Sanctuary/ranch → training and
                                      welfare → transport, rescue,
                                      migration, and renewable materials.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

F. Creature-Material Ethics and Law

-   Wild herd corridors cannot be fenced without approved crossings.

-   Eggs, live feathers, horns, and companion bodies are not ordinary
    loot.

-   Working creatures require load, rest, habitat, and retirement rules.

-   Legendary Prairie Colossus relationships use accord and stewardship,
    not ownership.

G. Approval Questions

-   Which creature is the zone mascot and first regional recruitment?

-   Are working-animal enterprises controllable by players?

-   How much breeding detail is exposed to gameplay?

-   Does the Prairie Colossus appear as one individual, a species, or
    both?

Proposed Decisions

-   Every proposed-zone creature is recruitable through at least one
    species-appropriate path.

-   Renewable and recovery-based creature materials are standard; lethal
    harvesting is not the default economy.

-   Intelligent, spirit-active, legendary, and construct beings require
    consent, covenant, contract, or personhood-aware rules.

-   Each zone map must show habitats, migrations, sanctuaries,
    recruitment sites, resource sources, processing, storage, trade,
    restriction, depletion, and restoration.

-   Rare materials create specialization and provenance rather than
    automatic numerical superiority.

-   All content in this bible remains Proposed until the related zone is
    approved.
