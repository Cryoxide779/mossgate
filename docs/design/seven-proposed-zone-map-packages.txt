THE FALL OF MOSSGATE

STUDIO EDITION

PROPOSED TERRITORY ATLAS

Seven Zone Map Packages

Golden Plains • Silverwood Wilds • Verdant Coast • Emerald Isles
Saltsong Desert • Frostveil Expanse • Shattered Crown

Atlas Control

This document develops the seven territories positioned beyond the
approved core progression ending at The Sundering Scar. Every package is
intentionally marked Proposed. The layouts are detailed enough for
review, blockout planning, and continent reconciliation, but none
becomes canon until approved through Chapter 31 governance.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  MAP PACKAGE PROMISE
  Each zone receives a practical geography, settlement network, routes,
  travel scale, dungeons, resource layers, creature habitats, recruitment
  geography, world-state variations, and a list of required detailed
  maps.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. The Golden Plains

Wide Horizons. Enduring People.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  STATUS: PROPOSED — NOT YET CANONICAL
  A broad interior territory proposed as the first expansion beyond the
  approved core. It must justify its size through mounted travel,
  agriculture, herds, caravans, settlement growth, and visible supply
  relationships with the rest of Ashveil Reach.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Regional Purpose and Gameplay Identity

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Map Control                         Definition
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Primary Fantasy                     Cross immense grasslands where
                                      farms, herds, roads, and moving
                                      communities determine regional
                                      life.

  Gameplay Pillars                    Mounted traversal, caravan
                                      protection, agriculture, creature
                                      herding, weather reading,
                                      open-field encounters, and
                                      settlement projects.

  Continental Role                    Primary grain, fiber, livestock,
                                      leather, wagon, and provisioning
                                      exporter.

  Player Progression                  Transitions from road-level travel
                                      to regional logistics and land
                                      stewardship.

  Visual Identity                     Golden grasses, wide river valleys,
                                      windbreak groves, distant mesas,
                                      storm fronts, mills, silos, and
                                      long roads.

  Map Risk                            Empty scale. The map must maintain
                                      meaningful travel rhythm without
                                      filling every field with
                                      encounters.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

B. Canonical Scale and Spatial Structure

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Measurement                         Proposed Value / Rule
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Regional Footprint                  Approximately 180 km east-west by
                                      135 km north-south for planning;
                                      subject to continent
                                      reconciliation.

  Major Hub Spacing                   35–55 km between major settlements;
                                      12–25 km between villages, ranches,
                                      and waystations.

  Vertical Range                      Low basin near 140 m; upland rim
                                      near 1,100 m.

  Travel Rhythm                       Safe or serviced stop every 12–18

  Water Logic                         Two major rivers, seasonal
                                      tributaries, irrigation canals,
                                      prairie ponds, and drought basins.

  Border Connections                  Core territories west/northwest;
                                      Verdant Coast south; Silverwood
                                      Wilds southeast; Saltsong

2. Silverwood Wilds

Untamed Beauty. Hidden Wonders.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  STATUS: PROPOSED — NOT YET CANONICAL
  A deep southern wilderness proposed as a counterpart to Whisper Woods.
  Unlike the stewarded and spiritually legible Whisper Woods, Silverwood
  must feel expansive, poorly mapped, highly biodiverse, and resistant to
  permanent settlement.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Regional Purpose and Gameplay Identity

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Map Control                         Definition
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Primary Fantasy                     Enter an enormous living wilderness
                                      where routes are learned through
                                      ecology, creatures, stars, rivers,
                                      and local knowledge.

  Gameplay Pillars                    Expedition planning, rare creature
                                      recruitment, field research,
                                      sanctuary creation, climbing, river
                                      traversal, and low-impact camps.

  Continental Role                    Rare medicine, flexible woods,
                                      resins, dyes, spirit-active flora,
                                      and conservation knowledge.

  Player Progression                  Tests independence from formal
                                      roads and services.

  Visual Identity                     Silver-barked canopy, deep green
                                      wetlands, luminous glades, massive
                                      roots, mist waterfalls, and
                                      vertical forest layers.

  Map Risk                            Overlap with Whisper Woods.
                                      Identity must emphasize remoteness,
                                      navigation uncertainty, canopy
                                      layers, and temporary rather than
                                      urban infrastructure.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

B. Canonical Scale and Spatial Structure

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Measurement                         Proposed Value / Rule
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Regional Footprint                  Approximately 150 km by 150 km for
                                      planning; fragmented by rivers and
                                      inaccessible canopy.

  Mapped Certainty                    Only 45–60% reliably mapped at
                                      entry; player and faction maps
                                      differ.

  Vertical Range                      River lowlands near 80 m;
                                      escarpments and canopy ridges near
                                      1,600 m.

  Travel Rhythm                       Camps and sanctuaries every 15–30
                                      km, but routes change seasonally.

  Water Logic                         One major river, three tributary
                                      systems, falls, sink pools, flooded
                                      forest, and hidden springs.

  Border Connections                  Golden Plains west; Verdant Coast
                                      south; core forest systems north;
                                      uncertain eastern boundary.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

3. The Verdant Coast

Cliffs, Currents, and Cultures.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  STATUS: PROPOSED — NOT YET CANONICAL
  A proposed continental coast focused on ports, trade, cliff
  settlements, fisheries, diplomacy, and coastal hazards. It must remain
  distinct from Misty Fen Lake by emphasizing open sea, tidal cliffs,
  international routes, and larger maritime infrastructure.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Regional Purpose and Gameplay Identity

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Map Control                         Definition
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Primary Fantasy                     Travel a dramatic inhabited
                                      coastline where ports, cliff roads,
                                      sea caves, fisheries, and foreign
                                      trade shape daily life.

  Gameplay Pillars                    Maritime commerce, cliff traversal,
                                      sailing, coastal rescue, diplomacy,
                                      storms, fisheries, and port
                                      development.

  Continental Role                    Major export coast and gateway to
                                      the Emerald Isles and distant
                                      lands.

  Player Progression                  Expands lake boating into
                                      open-water navigation and
                                      multinational logistics.

  Visual Identity                     White limestone cliffs, green
                                      terraces, blue coves, sea arches,
                                      lighthouse chains, storm walls, and
                                      dense ports.

  Map Risk                            Redundancy with Misty Fen Lake. The
                                      coast must emphasize tidal systems,
                                      ocean swells, cliff elevation, and
                                      international traffic.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

K. Approval Questions

-   Is the region a former unified kingdom, empire, or confederation?

-   Does a legitimate hereditary claim exist, or is legitimacy
    intentionally unresolved?

-   Can the player influence monarchy, coalition, commonwealth, or
    permanent fragmentation?

-   What historical relationship connects the Crown to The Sundering
    Scar?

-   How much restoration can permanently alter Crownspire and the
    aqueduct network?

Cross-Zone Reconciliation

The seven proposed territories should be reconciled together before any
individual region is approved. Roads, watersheds, mountain chains,
trade, climate, cultures, creature migration, and historical
infrastructure must connect across borders.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Reconciliation Layer                Required Decision
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Continent Placement                 Lock each territory’s position
                                      relative to the approved seven
                                      regions.

  Watersheds                          Ensure rivers originate, flow, and
                                      terminate believably across
                                      borders.

  Mountain Chains                     Connect Frostveil, Shattered Crown,
                                      Old Watchtower, Stonebridge, and
                                      desert rain shadows.

  Climate                             Define prevailing winds, coasts,
                                      currents, rainfall, temperature,
                                      and seasonal transitions.

  Trade                               Map food, metal, timber, salt,
                                      medicine, textiles, ships, and rare
                                      materials.

  Progression                         Separate launch, expansion,
                                      optional, high-risk, and endgame
                                      territories.

  Culture                             Prevent each biome from being
                                      treated as one uniform culture.

  Creatures                           Map migrations and species variants
                                      without breaking the recruitability
                                      invariant.

  History                             Connect ruins, roads, wars,
                                      migrations, and The Sundering Scar
                                      without forcing one explanation.

  Technical Scope                     Estimate region size, streaming,
                                      travel, persistence, and
                                      world-state costs before approval.
