THE FALL OF MOSSGATE: STUDIO EDITION
ASHVEIL REACH MATERIAL USAGE FRAMEWORK

Document Purpose
This document consolidates the current material-usage framework for The Fall of MossGate. It connects raw resources, processing, crafting, construction, regional identity, trade, creature welfare, provenance, repair, recycling, and detailed map development.

======================================================================
1. MATERIAL DESIGN RULE
======================================================================

Every harvestable material needs seven connected definitions:

1. Source
   - Where the material comes from.

2. Harvest Method
   - Mining, logging, gathering, hunting, salvaging, farming, cultivating, cleansing, negotiating, or another approved method.

3. Processing
   - Smelting, tanning, drying, milling, weaving, refining, carving, stabilizing, enchanting, or another required transformation.

4. Primary Properties
   - Weight, hardness, flexibility, conductivity, insulation, resonance, durability, toxicity, rarity, and other defining characteristics.

5. Gameplay Uses
   - Weapons, armor, buildings, tools, medicine, rituals, creature equipment, vehicles, trade, or other practical applications.

6. Regional Importance
   - Which zones produce, consume, export, restrict, regulate, or fight over the material.

7. Lifecycle
   - Damage, repair, recycling, contamination, degradation, inheritance, provenance, and end-of-life handling.

2. METALS AND ORES
======================================================================

IRON

Properties:
- Common.
- Strong.
- Moderately heavy.
- Easy to repair.
- Accepts many finishes and enchantments.

Uses:
- Weapons.
- Armor.
- Nails and brackets.
- Tools.
- Wagon hardware.
- Gates.
- Chains.
- Companion barding.
- Construction reinforcement.

Primary Producers:
- Stonebridge.
- Old Watchtower.
- The Shattered Crown.

Design Role:
Iron establishes the baseline against which advanced metals are measured. Iron should never become useless merely because rarer materials exist.

STEEL

Steel is a processed material rather than a raw resource.

Properties Depend On:
- Ore quality.
- Carbon source.
- Furnace temperature.
- Quenching liquid.
- Heat treatment.
- Smithing quality.
- Component geometry.

Uses:
- Professional weapon components.
- Medium and heavy armor.
- Springs.
- Mechanisms.
- Tools.
- Fortifications.
- Vehicle parts.
- Reinforced creature equipment.

Regional Specializations:
- Stonebridge: industrial and standardized steel.
- Old Watchtower: defensive and battlefield steel.
- The Shattered Crown: recovered royal steel.
- Saltsong Desert: heat-treated desert steel.

COPPER

Properties:
- Soft.
- Corrosion resistant.
- Highly conductive.
- Easy to shape.
- Strong magical and electrical routing potential.

Uses:
- Conduits.
- Alchemical equipment.
- Decorative construction.
- Plumbing.
- Cooking vessels.
- Communication devices.
- Lightning equipment.
- Ritual circles.
- Creature-sensing harnesses.

Primary Producers:
- Stonebridge.
- The Golden Plains.
- Saltsong Desert.

BRONZE

Properties:
- Durable.
- Corrosion resistant.
- Easier to cast than steel.
- Well suited to marine and ceremonial applications.

Uses:
- Coastal armor.
- Ship fittings.
- Bells.
- Statues.
- Ritual equipment.
- Shields.
- Polearm heads.
- Public monuments.
- Construct components.

Important Regions:
- The Verdant Coast.
- Emerald Isles.
- Misty Fen Lake.
- Saltsong Desert.

SILVER

Properties:
- Conducts certain Sacred, spiritual, and cleansing effects.
- Soft without alloying.
- Valuable.
- Visually identifiable.
- Useful against specific supernatural states.

Uses:
- Exorcist tools.
- Spirit-contact equipment.
- Ritual wire.
- Weapon inlays.
- Medical instruments.
- Identification seals.
- Anti-corruption containers.
- Funerary objects.

Important Regions:
- The Shattered Crown.
- Whisper Woods.
- Old Watchtower.
- Frostveil Expanse.

Design Rule:
Silver is not a universal monster-killing metal. Effectiveness depends on the target, preparation, alloy, ritual, and weapon construction.

GOLD

Properties:
- Soft.
- Corrosion resistant.
- Highly workable.
- Valuable.
- Strong ceremonial and magical-routing use.

Uses:
- Sacred conduits.
- Authority seals.
- Currency reserves.
- Jewelry.
- High-status commissions.
- Enchantment channels.
- Diplomatic gifts.
- Memorial objects.

Important Regions:
- The Golden Plains.
- Saltsong Desert.
- The Shattered Crown.

Design Rule:
Gold normally supports a stronger structural material rather than replacing it.

FROSTIRON

Status: Proposed regional material.

Source:
- Frostveil Expanse glacial deposits.
- Cold-forged northern ruins.

Properties:
- Retains cold.
- Resists brittle failure at low temperatures.
- Difficult to heat and forge.
- Dampens heat-based effects.

Uses:
- Cold-weather tools.
- Ice-cutting weapons.
- Heat-resistant containers.
- Cold-storage equipment.
- Frost wards.
- Mountain equipment.

Weakness:
Poorly treated Frostiron can become unstable in extreme heat.

CROWNSTEEL

Status: Proposed historical alloy.

Source:
- Royal armories.
- Broken fortress gates.
- Burial vaults.
- Shattered Crown ruins.

Properties:
- Exceptional structural memory.
- Durable under repeated repair.
- Often carries historical resonance.
- Difficult to reforge without losing provenance.

Uses:
- Heirloom weapons.
- Restoration projects.
- Authority seals.
- Defensive equipment.
- Memorial items.
- Successor equipment.

SHARDMETAL

Source:
- Metal altered by prolonged Shard exposure.
- Stabilized through controlled alloying with ordinary metals.

Properties:
- High magical conductivity.
- Unpredictable resonance.
- Can amplify multiple magical sources.
- Can contaminate nearby components.

Uses:
- Advanced conduits.
- Shard-resistant equipment.
- Research tools.
- Exorcist devices.
- High-risk weapons.
- Containment mechanisms.

Requirements:
- Stabilization.
- Provenance.
- Containment rating.
- Exposure record.
- Legal handling status.

======================================================================
16. DECISIONS TO LOCK
======================================================================

- Ordinary materials remain relevant through repairability, accessibility, familiarity, and regional supply.
- Rare materials create specialization rather than automatic superiority.
- Material properties come from source, treatment, geometry, quality, condition, and component role.
- Every material has harvesting, processing, maintenance, and end-of-life rules.
- Creature materials can be gathered through renewable, nonlethal, ecological, and recovery-based methods.
- Creature recruitment remains an alternative to killing creatures for components.
- Provenance follows rare, historical, creature-derived, contaminated, Sacred, and Shard materials.
- Regional materials shape architecture, clothing, trade, professions, roads, settlements, and political relationships.
- Salvage and recycling are complete production systems.
- Resource locations must be included while developing each detailed zone map.
