THE FALL OF MOSSGATE

MossGate kept the roads open, the coin honest, and the old seals shut. It sat on the greatest crossing of the Veinways, and for generations it held.

Then it failed. Alarms rang before any attack was seen. Sealed districts opened from within. Trusted signals gave contradictory orders. Survivors call it the Gatefall.

Now the Ash Road is crowded with refugees bound for CrestHollow, and what escaped is following them. Your caravan has just been hit.
