**Full description** (4000 max)
The fortress-city of MossGate fell in a single night. Its seals opened from the inside, its roads broke, and whatever was held beneath it got out. You arrive on the Ash Road with the survivors, bound for CrestHollow and whatever is left to save.

MossGate is a hand-crafted pixel-art side-scrolling RPG with no classes. You become what you practice.

• No class lock-in. Pick a starter kit, then train any of a dozen disciplines: blades, archery, nature magic, necromancy, armor, gathering and crafting all grow with use.
• Real combat. Roll through attacks, block with stamina, and time a parry to stagger enemies and reflect their shots. Break an enemy's poise to stun it.
• A living region. Towns with people who remember what you did, day and night, weather, music, and regional crises that grow worse if no one answers them.
• Every creature can be a companion. Wear one down or approach a gentle one, offer the food it likes, and earn its trust. Companions level up, learn commands and unlock special abilities.
• The Veinway Deep. A 100-floor dungeon under CrestHollow, with a guardian every ten floors and anchors every five so you can return to your deepest point.
• A full story across three chapters, from the wreck of your caravan to the three keepers who held MossGate's seals.
• Gather, craft and trade. Mine ore, fell timber, fish the fens, forge your own gear with your name on it, and take contracts and freight runs for the towns.
• Plays offline. No ads, no data collection.

Free updates add new zones, story and features over time.
